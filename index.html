Choose the right foam

Open cell vs. closed cell

Not sure which one you need? We'll recommend the right foam for each area during your free estimate.

Open Cell Foam

Best for attics & interior walls
  • Expands to fill every gap and crack
  • Excellent sound dampening
  • Most budget-friendly per square foot
  • Roof stays breathable for leak detection

R-3.7per inch

~0.5 lbdensity

Closed Cell Foam

Best for crawl spaces, basements & metal
  • Built-in vapor barrier — blocks moisture
  • Adds structural rigidity
  • Highest R-value per inch
  • Stops condensation on metal buildings

R-6.5+per inch

~2 lbdensity

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How much does spray foam insulation cost?

It depends on the area, foam type and thickness. Most projects are priced per square foot. Use our 3D calculator to get your square footage, then we'll give you an exact written price at no cost.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. We come out, inspect, measure and give you a written quote with no obligation.

How long does installation take?

Most attics and crawl spaces are completed in one day. Larger buildings and new construction may take a few days.

Can I stay in my home during the job?

We recommend staying out of the home during spraying and for about 24 hours after, while the foam cures and the area is ventilated. We'll give you clear instructions for your project.

Do you insulate metal buildings and shops?

Yes. Closed cell foam is ideal for metal buildings — it insulates and stops condensation on the steel panels. Our 3D calculator was built for exactly these buildings.
